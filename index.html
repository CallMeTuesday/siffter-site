Three keys.
That's the whole app.

←
Reject

Swipe left or press the left arrow. The file moves to a folder you choose. Nothing is deleted.

⌘ Z
Undo

Puts the last file back exactly where it was. Your progress saves after every decision.

→
Keep

Swipe right or press the right arrow. The file stays where it is, unless you choose a folder for kept files.

All your music,
one review.

Apple Music, music folders and sample folders, side by side. MP3, AAC, FLAC, WAV and AIFF, wherever you keep them.

gets deleted.

Rejects wait in a folder you pick. Nothing leaves Apple Music until you approve, and ⌘Z puts anything back exactly where it was.
