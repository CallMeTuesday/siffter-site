Syfter

Keep the tracks you love.
Clear out the rest.

For Apple Music, any folder of music, and your samples.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Requires macOS 14 or later.

Reject

Swipe left or press the left arrow. The file moves to a folder you choose. Nothing is deleted.

Keep

Swipe right or press the right arrow. The file stays where it is, unless you choose a folder for kept files.

Your Apple Music library

Review the songs downloaded to your Mac. Nothing changes in Apple Music until you approve the removals.

Any folder of music

Point Syfter at one or more folders. Each song starts partway in, so you hear the part that matters.

Your samples, too

Breaks, loops and one-shots. Anything under a minute plays from the start.

Undo anything

Press ⌘ Z to put the last file back exactly where it was. Your progress is saved after every decision.
