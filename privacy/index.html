Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

Siffter is made by TCTCTC LLC (“we”). This policy covers the Siffter app for Mac and this website.

The short version

Siffter does not collect, store or share any of your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app is not allowed to connect to the internet at all, so nothing about you or your library can leave your Mac through it.

What stays on your Mac

To do its job, Siffter keeps a few things in its own private storage on your Mac:

  • The folders you choose, and macOS permission to keep using them.
  • Your progress: which files you have kept or rejected, and the history that lets you undo.
  • Apple Music removals you have queued or approved, with a record that lets them be restored.
  • Your settings.

We never see any of this. Deleting Siffter removes it.

Apple Music

If you turn on Apple Music, macOS asks you to allow Siffter to access your media library and to control the Music app. Siffter uses that access only to list the songs downloaded to your Mac and, when you approve it, to remove the songs you rejected from your library. It does not read your listening history, your account or anything streamed.

Your files

Siffter plays the audio files you choose, reads their tags and artwork to show them, and moves the files you reject to a folder you pick. It never deletes your audio.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These come from Apple under its own privacy policy, and we use them only to fix bugs.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

The site’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads. See Google’s privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we do not share them.

Children

Siffter collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date above.
